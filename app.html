Srcset Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out responsive image widths from the numbers you type. It has not seen your images or your page. It does not know your image quality, your file sizes or your layout, how a browser will really choose between files, or whether the pictures will look good, and it gives no verdict that a set of files is right. Every number is your own entry.

The need truth

The pixels across that an image slot needs on a screen. The slot is in CSS px.

The ladder truth

The file widths for 1x screens up to the top density you pick, using the slot width above.

The pick truth

Which of your listed files is the smallest one wide enough, using the slot width and density above. A browser may choose differently.

Srcset Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
